Drop = amps × (2 × feet) × ohms per 1,000 ft ÷ 1,000. The 2 is because current goes out and back. Assumes the whole load is at the far end, which is the worst case, so real runs with devices spread along them do a little better.

Wire resistance: NEC Chapter 9, Table 8, uncoated copper at 75 °C (ohms per 1,000 ft, solid / stranded): 18 AWG 7.77 / 7.95, 16 AWG 4.89 / 4.99, 14 AWG 3.07 / 3.14, 12 AWG 1.93 / 1.98, 10 AWG 1.21 / 1.24. 22 AWG isn't in Table 8, so it isn't offered. For fire NACs, start from the panel's minimum NAC output voltage in its manual rather than the nominal 24 V.

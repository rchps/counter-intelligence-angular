Amp-hours = (standby amps × standby hours + alarm amps × alarm minutes ÷ 60) × safety factor, rounded up to the next common battery size.

NFPA 72 §10.6.7.2.1: 24 hours standby, then 5 minutes of alarm; in-building emergency voice/alarm communications: 24 hours, then 15 minutes at maximum load. Safety factor: ×1.25 in the 2022 edition (aging correction), ×1.20 in the 2010–2019 editions (20% margin). Fire-Lite and other panel worksheets use the same method.
